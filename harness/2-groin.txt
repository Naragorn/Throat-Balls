# A punch between the legs: he bends over with his hands on his groin for Groin Duration.
# The contact point is the lower torso part (the hips) moved 8 cm down and 8 cm forward, the
# groin zone's center with the default offsets. If the mod's hips part (the ragdoll's root) is
# not this part, the SKIP line names the distance to the groin zone.
wait-level
set-option ThroatBalls "Throat Balls Enabled" true
set-option ThroatBalls "Debug Logging" true
set-option ThroatBalls "Own Sounds" true
# Chances pinned: a run must not depend on a roll.
set-option ThroatBalls "Throat Reaction Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Reaction Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Knockdown Chance" 0
set-option ThroatBalls "Both Hits Knockdown Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Allowed Hits" "Fists, Kicks, Blunt Weapons"
set-option ThroatBalls "Metal Armor Protects" false
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Duration" 4
set-option ThroatBalls "Drop Weapons" WhenTheyGoDown
frame-rate 90

spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 facing=0 brain=HumanDummy
record-start groin fps=30 width=960 height=540 views=npc distance=1.8 rise=1.0 angle=60
mark
hit-npc Torso section=Lower at=0,-0.08,0.08 type=Blunt velocity=5
expect-log [ThroatBalls] LOW BLOW
expect-log | Groin start
# Own Sounds: the groin file once at the start; the repeats stay the game's heavy breathing (Groan Interval 3 s).
expect-log | own Groin 'groin-test.wav'
wait 1
screenshot groin-bent of=npc distance=1.8 height=1.0 angle=60
await-log 6 | Groin end (time up)
expect-log | heavybreath
wait 1
record-stop

# A groin hit alone takes him down at Groin Knockdown Chance 100 % (Nadi 2026-11-22: 50 % by default),
# and never at 0 % (the first part of this scenario).
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Knockdown Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Fall Style" CurlUp
set-option ThroatBalls "Down Duration" 4
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 angle=-60 facing=0 brain=HumanDummy
record-start groin-down fps=30 width=960 height=540 views=npc distance=2 rise=1.0 angle=60
mark
hit-npc Torso section=Lower at=0,-0.08,0.08 type=Blunt velocity=5
expect-log | Down start (groin
expect-log | own Groin 'groin-test.wav'
wait 2.5
screenshot groin-down of=npc distance=2 height=1.0 angle=60
await-log 4 | Down end (time up)
wait 2
record-stop
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Knockdown Chance" 0.5
set-option ThroatBalls "Down Duration" 8
expect-no-log Exception
quit
