# A punch on the Adam's apple: he grabs his throat for Throat Duration, then fights on.
# Refused: a punch on the chest (outside both zones), a slash on the throat (Blunt Only),
# a punch from behind (Max Hit Angle). The contact point is the neck part moved 5 cm up and
# 5 cm forward, the throat zone's center with the default offsets (BasHarness hit-npc at=).
wait-level
set-option ThroatBalls "Throat Balls Enabled" true
set-option ThroatBalls "Debug Logging" true
# Chances pinned: a run must not depend on a roll.
set-option ThroatBalls "Throat Reaction Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Reaction Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Groin Knockdown Chance" 0
set-option ThroatBalls "Both Hits Knockdown Chance" 1
set-option ThroatBalls "Allowed Hits" BluntOnly
set-option ThroatBalls "Metal Armor Protects" false
set-option ThroatBalls "Men Only" true
set-option ThroatBalls "Throat Duration" 3
set-option ThroatBalls "Both Hits Take Him Down" true

spawn-npc HumanMale distance=1.5 facing=0 brain=HumanDummy
mark
hit-npc Neck at=0,0.05,0.05 type=Blunt velocity=5
expect-log [ThroatBalls] LOW BLOW
expect-log | Throat start
wait 0.8
screenshot throat-hands of=npc distance=1.6 height=1.5 angle=0
await-log 5 | Throat end (time up)
# The brain wakes after the stun clip's end; a HumanDummy must stay without its behaviour tree.
wait 2
expect-npc tree False within=0.1

mark
hit-npc Torso section=Upper at=0,0,0.15 type=Blunt velocity=5
expect-log | OutsideZones
mark
hit-npc Neck at=0,0.05,0.05 type=Slash velocity=5
expect-log | HitNotAllowed
expect-no-log | Throat start
expect-no-log Exception
quit
