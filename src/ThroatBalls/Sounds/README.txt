Throat Balls: sounds

throat-choke.wav comes with the mod: the choke when he is hit on the throat.
Sound: ZapSplat (https://www.zapsplat.com). Delete it if you only want your own.

Put your own WAV files into this folder:
- throat*.wav (for example throat-gag.wav): he chokes when hit on the throat, and again while it lasts.
- groin*.wav (for example groin-ow.wav): he cries out once when hit between the legs.

Several files per spot are fine. They take turns, the same one never twice in a row.
Save them as WAV (16-bit PCM is safest). MP3 and OGG are not read.
No files, or Own Sounds off in the Mod Menu: the game's sounds play.
The files are read when the game starts.
